Key Cybersecurity Awareness Concepts

  • Social Engineering Lures: Attractive, harmless-looking interactive pages (such as love calculators, greetings, or quizzes) are frequently used to entice visitors into interacting with links.
  • Battery Status API Vulnerability: Demonstrates why the W3C Battery Status API was restricted in Firefox and Safari after security researchers proved it could track users across private browsing sessions.
  • Digital Fingerprinting Entropy: Combining display dimensions, OS build, timezone, and user-agent strings creates a high-entropy profile that can identify a specific visitor across websites without cookies.
